I’m a freelance writer based in Austin, Texas, spending most of time with Texas history these days. My work has appeared in publications, such as Texas Monthly Magazine, The New York Times, The Washington Post, Texas Highways, The Guardian, NPR, Preservation Magazine, and more. I worked previously for The Texas Tribune from 2013 to 2023.

My work has directly influenced state policymaking, and it has been repeatedly recognized, including with a national Edward R. Murrow Award.
